# Abstract

Motion transfer is an important task in video editing with numerous practical applications. Current leading approaches fine-tune a small scale of parameters, e.g., LoRA, on a single source video to adapt the video diffusion model (VDM) to its motion. However, such single-video fine-tuning makes it challenging to ensure the physical realism of intrinsic motion. For example, when editing a car to a horse, the LoRA tends to memorize the rotation of the car's tires, resulting in the horse's legs appearing stiff, and the overall posture is nearly unchanged. To this end, we propose a **Dual grained Re-Adaptation Motion Transfer (DRA-MTransfer)** method, which utilizes the potential motion prior within the pre-trained VDM to tackle the motion constraint imposed by the source video. 

First, to address posture stiffness, we propose a **spatial consistency guided re-weighting (SCR)** module to activate VDM in a coarse grained manner. SCR employs Gaussian re-weighting to alleviate the spatial constraint imposed by the source video during training, and adaptively activates the weight of VDM guided by spatial consistency during inference. 

Next, to improve the local motion details, we propose a **synergistic temporal re-attention (STR)** module to utilize intrinsic motion pattern of the edited subject within VDM in a fine grained manner. Specifically, STR integrates the motion-related features from VDM into temporal attention to enrich intrinsic motion patterns, and sharpens the attention maps to mitigate artifacts. 

Extensive qualitative and quantitative (at least + 4%) experiments demonstrate the superiority of DRA-MTransfer in intrinsic-aware motion transfer.
