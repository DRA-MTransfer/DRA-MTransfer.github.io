# Pipeline

![](./pipeline/pipeline.jpg)

- **Spatial Consistency Guided Re-Weighting (SCR)**: During training, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{G}}$ uses Gaussian distribution to smooth the constraint of LoRA. During inference, temporal LoRA weight ($\omega^t$) is adaptively adjusted by the spatial consistency of foreground masks, and spatial LoRA weight ($\omega^s$) is determined by a classifier model based on the prompt pair and weighting dictionary.
- **Synergistic Temporal Re-Attention (STR)**: During inference, Foreground Enhanced Attention (FEA) temporally integrates keys and values from VDM and VDM-A to utilize correct **intrinsic motion pattern (IMP)**.
