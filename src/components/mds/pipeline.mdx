# Pipeline

![](./pipeline/pipeline-new.jpg)

- **Spatial Consistency Guided Re-Weighting (SCR)**: During training, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{G}}$ uses Gaussian distribution to re-weighting the loss on spatial consistency. During inference, temporal LoRA weight ($\omega^t$) is adaptively adjusted by spatial consistency, and spatial LoRA weight ($\omega^s$) is determined by a classifier model based on the prompt pair and weighting dictionary.
- **Synergistic Temporal Re-Attention (STR)**: During inference, Foreground Enhanced Attention (FEA) integrates the IMP knowledge of edited subject within VDM into VDM-A by concatenating their keys and values.
